# Changelog

## Current beta

- Place picture: a new optional input of every mode (under the reference video). Given, it is the place of every shot instead of the places the planner would make, and no plate is painted (`place` in the job request, slots `place_image` and `place_on` of the shot templates; absent, its LoadImage is taken out and the generated places are used). It travels with presets, projects and archives like the other inputs.
- The graphs the templates are exported from are the pack's `HyperKut Creative Text`, `HyperKut Creative Music` and `HyperKut Remix` (called SECATOR before): `scripts/export_templates.js` reads those names, defines `window.hyperkutExport` (`window.secatorExport` stays) and writes `hyperkut_<mode>_<template>.json`.
- No more framed paintings in unrelated shots. With places on, the empty place picture is a reference of every shot, and the shot templates still asked it for "mounted artwork/posters" and a "fully set-dressed" place: FLUX.2 klein painted two framed posters on almost every plate, and the plate check let them pass. The plate prompt and its check now take the wording of the pack graphs (plain walls, nothing hung unless the place names it), and the plate is painted from the seed of the job (`plate_seed`) instead of the fixed seed 7, so one job no longer gets the plate of every other.
- The location plates are painted by MiniMax H3 Image (ComfyUI 0.45, `MiniMaxH3ImageConditioning`) instead of FLUX.2 klein: `minimax_h3_image_pruned_int8_convrot` and `minimax_h3_image_vae_fp16`, the text encoder of H3 the shot already loads, 6 steps on the distilled grid of the checkpoint, 1088 x 1920 in Text and Music and the size of the shot in Remix. Without those files the shots are still made, without location images, and the user is told. The FLUX files are no longer needed.
- Drag and drop of the inputs: videos, pictures and sounds dropped anywhere on the inputs panel go to the first free slot of their kind (a video to the source of a Remix then to the reference video, pictures to the free reference slots in order, a sound to the music or the voice); several pictures dropped on a reference slot fill the free ones, and the file picker of a reference takes several. While dragging, the panel and the slot show whether the file can land there; a file with no slot of its kind is named in a message, and a file dropped beside the panel no longer makes the browser leave the Studio.
- API cost: approving a plan over the budget no longer lifts the cap. The approved amounts (seconds and dollars, with 5 % for rounding) become the cap of the job, every shot prompt of the job shares one budget in the pack (`budget_key` = the job), a regeneration has the budget of a run of its own, and a plan made again keeps its approval only when it asks for no more than what was approved. `POST /resume` and `/regenerate` refuse a plan waiting for its approval (only `/approve` takes it out of review), and a resumed project is priced again: one over the budget goes back to review. The estimate counts the second take and the retry (`takes`, `seconds_high`; the model card compares the budget with it), and a plan the pack cannot price waits for an approval instead of failing after the plan.
- Uploads reserve their room under the storage lock and are written outside it, so a large upload no longer makes the other uploads and the start of a job answer 503; the scan of the storage runs in a thread; an upload never takes the file name of the client (it is written as `<id>.part`, and the parts a stop left behind are removed at start).
- `object_info` is read once a minute at most, the node schemas of a deployment are read again after a day (a release at the same address), the library asks the sizes of the projects only when a job ends, appears or goes and the server keeps them until a project changes, and the stream of a job always sends its last state. `/api/health` says `pack_changed` when the VideoTools pack was updated after Studio loaded it. `COMFY_BASE_URL` must be HTTPS unless it is this computer (the API key is sent to it). The jury quota is written in a thread. Interface: an empty answer is no error, an unreachable Studio is said in words, and the video model cards are one Tab stop moved with the arrows.
- The video model is the first thing of the form, with its price: one card per model (H3 free on this computer, Seedance, Wan, LTX with what an edit of the length asked would cost, from the price badges of the Studio's ComfyUI), then the resolution, the budgets and an estimate that says whether the edit is within them. It replaces the lines that were hidden in the Quality and tools group. New `GET /api/video-models/estimate` (mode, seconds, ratio, resolution, refs, ref_video, music); the estimate counts shots of 3 s on average and the plan still shows the exact figure.
- Remix with Seedance 2.5: the video model choice is in the Remix tab too (H3, or Seedance in edit mode, which changes the source shot and keeps its length and format; Wan and LTX do not edit a video and are not offered). The Remix shot template got the router and the plan to approve shows the cost of the edits. Keep H3 for a shot under two seconds or for pose-only remixes.
- Video model for Text and Music: H3 on this computer (the default), or Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0 or LTX 2.5 through the Comfy API. Quality and tools holds the model, the API resolution and a budget (seconds of video and dollars per run). The plan to approve shows the estimate (the price badges of the API nodes added up, with `jsonata-python`) and a plan over the budget waits for approval even when the plan review is off; approving lifts the cap for that project. The API templates of Text and Music got the model choice by `scripts/add_generator_router.py` (patched, not exported again, so nothing they hold is lost); the shot's prompt is written in the style of the model. Needs the VideoTools commit pinned in CI.
- Hit sync, a fourth editing rhythm of the Music tab and the options: cuts on the kicks, the bass, the snares and the voice. Public instance: the status, health and storage routes no longer tell a stranger anything about the host. A Remix shot of a fifth of a second or less is generated on at least 22 frames and cut back to its length.
- The pictures and music of the app's presets ship with it (`presets/inputs`), so a preset loads its files on any machine, a remote Studio included, and every jury visitor can use them. The source videos of the Remix presets are not shipped: a placeholder shows where to add one, and no generation starts until it is replaced.
- Remix gets the creative tools of Text and Music. A remix planner (creative or transfer strategy, or casting on) reads the whole source and writes the story line of each shot, who of the pictures replaces whom, the places and the shots to keep as in the source or turn into a title card, without changing the cuts. Also: best of two takes judged against the source shot, visual memory of each person, location images, camera moves for a creative remix, transcription of the source for kinetic lyrics, three Remix treatments (faithful, twist, another story), and a "Keep the original shot" toggle per shot. An older ComfyUI without the Remix Plan node makes the remix without its plan.
- The storage lock no longer freezes the Studio during an upload check, an import or an export; Clean caches also removes unused uploads and the pictures the Studio cut.
- Text and Music: story then casting, voice and lyric transcription, visual memory, camera moves, Another idea, three treatments, best of two takes (the second take now has its own seed), kinetic lyrics and the Pika H3 Flex option; see the VideoTools changelog for the nodes. Missing optional models or nodes (Pika Flex LoRA, FLUX location images, pose control, music model) are cut out of the prompt instead of stopping a generation.

- CI uses a matching VideoTools runtime with motion templates and checks lazy-loaded motion modules before running backend tests.
- Remix, Text and Music creation through local ComfyUI and the Comfy SDK.
- Project library, presets, five visual references and English/French interface.
- Per-shot prompts, regeneration, retained takes, source-window selection and export.
- Shared manual montage editor with trim/slip, cuts, take selection and undo/redo.
- Experimental unified graphics, text/effects and sound finishing, disabled by default.
- Official presentation and a separately labelled simulated README walkthrough.
- Automatic export check: duration, ratio, audio track and, for Remix, cut count against the reference are compared with the request; findings show as warnings.
- Planned text is drawn by the assembler when HyperFrames is not available.
- In a remote or jury Studio the SFX library must sit inside `HYPERKUT_SFX_ROOT`.
- New projects default to adaptive rhythm, automatic cut pace and 100% musical synchronization.
- The job waveform reads audio in blocks instead of loading it whole.
